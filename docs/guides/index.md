# UC4 Editor

The UC4 Editor is a browser app for the Faderfox UC4. With it you can:

- **edit** the 18 setups of your UC4 on screen, control by control,
- **back them up** as files on your computer,
- **restore** them to the UC4 when you want them back.

Everything runs in your browser. Your setups stay on your computer, in this browser.

!!! info "Unofficial"
    The UC4 Editor is not made by, endorsed by or supported by Faderfox.

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): the browser you need, and connecting the UC4.
2. [The editor](editor.md): the screen, step by step.
3. [From and to the UC4](device.md): receive setups from the UC4 and send them back.

!!! warning "Sending overwrites setups"
    Sending a setup to the UC4 replaces what is stored there. Before you send anything, make a
    `SndA` backup of all 18 setups. The page [From and to the UC4](device.md) shows how.
